## Overview

## Location

The hotel is at 198, 31 Buakhaow 15, in Soi Buakhao. Soi Buakhao runs inland parallel to Second Road and is one of the busiest budget areas in the city, lined with open-air bars, guesthouses and street food. Buakhao Market and the Soi Diana junction are both within walking distance.

## Getting There

Suvarnabhumi Airport is roughly 120 km north — about 90 minutes by taxi, or two hours on the scheduled airport coaches, which cost a fraction of a private car.
