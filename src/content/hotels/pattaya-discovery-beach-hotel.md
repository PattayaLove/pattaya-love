## Overview

Pattaya Discovery Beach Hotel is a mid-range hotel in the Soi 6 area. It rates well overall, based on a large sample of 4,198 Google reviews (4.3/5).

## Location

You will find it at 489, Pattaya 6/1 in Soi 6. Soi 6 is a short street running between Beach Road and Second Road in north Pattaya, known almost entirely for its bar scene. The beach is at the western end, and Central Pattaya is a few minutes south by baht bus.

## Getting There

From Bangkok, buses to Pattaya leave Ekkamai terminal throughout the day. From Suvarnabhumi Airport a taxi takes about 90 minutes, and U-Tapao is nearer still at around 45 minutes.

## Guest-Friendly Policy

Most Pattaya hotels are guest-friendly and admit visitors without a joiner fee, but we have not independently confirmed the policy for Pattaya Discovery Beach Hotel. Check directly with the property before booking if this matters to you.

## Booking & Rates

Expect peak pricing between November and February. The low season from June to September brings the best rates alongside heavier afternoon rain. Mid-range rates in Pattaya swing widely with the calendar, so booking dates either side of a public holiday can make a substantial difference.
