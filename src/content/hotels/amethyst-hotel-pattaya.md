## Overview

Set in the Soi Buakhao area, Amethyst Hotel Pattaya is a mid-range hotel. It holds one of the stronger guest scores in its bracket — 4.6/5 from a moderate 403 Google reviews.

## Location

The address is 799 Buakhaow 15, Soi Buakhao. Soi Buakhao runs inland parallel to Second Road and is one of the busiest budget areas in the city, lined with open-air bars, guesthouses and street food. Buakhao Market and the Soi Diana junction are both within walking distance.

## Getting There

Suvarnabhumi Airport is roughly 120 km north — about 90 minutes by taxi, or two hours on the scheduled airport coaches, which cost a fraction of a private car.

## Booking & Rates

High season runs November to February and carries the highest rates; June to September is the cheapest window, with rain most likely in the afternoons. Mid-range rates in Pattaya swing widely with the calendar, so booking dates either side of a public holiday can make a substantial difference.
