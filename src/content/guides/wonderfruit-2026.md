Wonderfruit is five days of music, art, food, talks and camping in open fields in the hills behind Pattaya. It is the opposite of a beach party: no stage times you have to run between, programming somewhere on site around the clock, and a crowd that comes to stay for the whole thing.

The 2026 edition runs **Thursday 3 to Monday 7 December**, one week before [Tomorrowland Thailand](/guides/tomorrowland-pattaya-2026/). This guide covers which tickets are still available, how to get to The Fields, where to sleep, and how to do both festivals in one trip. Prices and availability last checked: **13 November 2026**, on the official Wonderfruit site.

\*Intro and Youth passes need an application, and approval is at the festival's sole discretion.

**Children aged 12 and under get in free** and do not need an advance ticket. They must be with a parent or guardian.

**At-door means subject to availability.** The festival's FAQ says remaining tickets *may* be available at the door after the November sale. Do not build a long-haul trip on a door ticket you have not secured.

**Upgrades at the box office:** Weekend → 5-Day THB 5,000; Sunday → Weekend THB 7,000; Sunday → 5-Day THB 10,000. Those upgrade prices are listed without the 7% VAT, and all upgrades are subject to availability.

For reference, the November online prices were THB 12,900 (5-Day) and THB 11,500 (Weekend), and October's were THB 11,900 and THB 10,500. Prices rise with every sale, which is why the door price is so much higher.

## Where The Fields Are

Wonderfruit takes place in **The Fields at Siam Country Club**, inland from the coast in Chon Buri. The festival's own shuttle page measures its pick-up points at 16 km (Na Jomtien), 22 km (Central Pattaya) and 24 km (Terminal 21 Pattaya) from The Fields. Treat it as a drive, not a walk from the beach.

## Getting There

### The official shuttle

This year the shuttle runs with **9-seater vans only**, no buses.

- **Pick-up points:** Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien Pattaya, Centre Point Prime Hotel Pattaya and Terminal 21 Pattaya — plus The Fields itself.
- **To The Fields:** 07:30 to midnight. **From The Fields:** 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** the page says vans leave every 40 minutes or when full; its FAQ says every 20 minutes. Plan for the longer wait.
- **Unlimited Shuttle Pass:** THB 1,800, on sale until 3 December. It is not sold at The Fields.
- **Walk-in:** THB 450 per single ride, subject to availability.

You get a QR code by email for your first ride, collect your wristband at the box office, and use the wristband for every ride after that. Seats are first come, first served, and you check in for each ride.

You do not need to stay at a partner hotel to use the shuttle. Get yourself to the nearest pick-up point by Grab or baht bus.

### Driving and scooters

- **Car parking:** buy in advance — THB 250 for a single entry, or THB 800 for four. On site it is THB 350 per vehicle if space is left, paid by QR code or PromptPay only. Each pass is one entry, no re-entry.
- **Motorbikes and scooters park free.**

If you are thinking about riding back at 3 a.m., read our [scooter guide](/guides/pattaya-scooter-rental/) first — licence and insurance rules apply here too, and night roads out of the fields are not the place to find out.

### From Bangkok and the airports

Wonderfruit itself points to the usual Bangkok–Pattaya options: buses, minivans, airport limousines, trains and rental cars. The simplest approach is to get to your Pattaya or Na Jomtien hotel first, then use the shuttle or a car for the last leg.

## Where to Stay

You have three realistic choices: on site, close to a shuttle stop, or inland near the venue.

### On site

Wonderfruit runs its own accommodation, sold separately from tickets:

- **Slow Wonder** — private cabins with ensuite bathrooms and air conditioning, check-in from 14:00 on 3 December.
- **Boutique Camping** — furnished pavilion tents, check-in from 12:00 on 3 December, check-out 12:00 on 7 December.
- **General Camping** — bring your own tent; no tents for rent. Space is first come, first served, and a camping fee covers showers, toilets and water refills.

Accommodation bookings cannot be changed once made, and you still need a festival ticket.

### Na Jomtien — closest shuttle stop

**Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien Pattaya** is an official partner hotel and the shuttle stop nearest to The Fields (16 km). Na Jomtien is the quiet beach south of Jomtien — a good base if you want the sea in the morning and the fields at night.

### Central and North Pattaya — near the other two stops

**Centre Point Prime Hotel Pattaya** (22 km) and **Terminal 21 Pattaya** (24 km) are the other pick-up points. Staying central is the right call if Pattaya's nightlife and restaurants are part of the trip.

### East Pattaya — inland, near Siam Country Club

[East Pattaya](/areas/east-pattaya/) is where expats live rather than where tourists stay, and Soi Siam Country Club is its main restaurant and bar road. It puts you on the inland side of the city, in the direction of The Fields, and rooms are usually cheaper than on the coast. There is no official shuttle stop here, so you will need your own transport or a Grab.

## Wonderfruit and Tomorrowland in One Trip

The two festivals sit one week apart, both in the hills east of Pattaya:

- **Wonderfruit:** Thursday 3 – Monday 7 December, The Fields at Siam Country Club
- **Tomorrowland Thailand:** Friday 11 – Sunday 13 December, Wisdom Valley

That leaves three days in between to recover — beach, [Koh Larn](/guides/koh-larn-island-guide/), sleep. Note that **Thursday 10 December is Constitution Day**, a Thai public holiday, so traffic and hotel demand pick up before Tomorrowland. Our [days around Tomorrowland](/guides/tomorrowland-pattaya-itinerary/) plan covers that week.

Tomorrowland is sold out; only the official waiting list and Ticket Exchange remain. Details in our [Tomorrowland tickets guide](/guides/tomorrowland-tickets/). ## What to Pack

Most of this comes straight from the festival's rules — read them, because security searches everyone at the gate.

- **A refillable bottle or a Wonder Cup.** Single-use plastic bottles and cups are banned everywhere on site, and drinking water is free at the water stations. The reusable Wonder Cup costs THB 690.
- **A battery pack.** There is no Wi-Fi, mobile signal can be patchy, and charging stations are at the water stations.
- **Some cash.** The site is cashless, but credit-card top-ups are non-refundable, so cash for topping up your wristband is useful. ATMs are on site.
- **ID.** Expect to show it for age checks at any time.
- **Earplugs** if you are camping — music runs into the early hours.
- **For General Camping:** tent, rain cover, tarp for shade, pegs and a mallet (the ground can be hard).

Leave at home: outside food and drink (it is confiscated, flasks are emptied), any alcohol, glass, drones and professional camera gear without a permit, selfie sticks, laughing gas, and anything illegal. Thai drug laws do not soften inside a festival.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Wonderfruit 2026?

Thursday 3 to Monday 7 December 2026, at The Fields at Siam Country Club in Chon Buri.

### Is Wonderfruit 2026 sold out?

The 5-Day Full Pass and the Weekend Pass are sold out online across all three sale windows. The tickets page lists at-door prices of THB 20,000 and THB 15,000, which the festival says depend on availability. The Sunday Pass, Teen Pass and Dog Pass were listed with prices on 13 November.

### How do I get from Pattaya to Wonderfruit?

By the official van shuttle from Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien, Centre Point Prime Hotel Pattaya or Terminal 21 Pattaya (THB 1,800 unlimited, or THB 450 per ride), or by car with a parking pass. Scooters park free.

### Can I leave and come back?

Yes, with a valid wristband, while the gates are open (08:00 to midnight). There is no entry between midnight and 08:00. If you leave after midnight on Sunday, you cannot get back in on Monday.

### Can I bring kids?

Yes. Children aged 12 and under get in free with a parent or guardian. Teens aged 13–19 need a Teen Pass.

### Can I do Wonderfruit and Tomorrowland in one trip?

Yes. Wonderfruit ends on Monday 7 December and Tomorrowland starts on Friday 11 December, so you get three days in between.
