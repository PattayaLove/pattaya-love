GoGo bars are the reason a lot of people come to Pattaya and the reason a lot of people are nervous about their first night here. The format is simple once you have seen it, the prices are lower than the reputation suggests, and the rules are unwritten but consistent. This guide explains all three, then walks you through the eleven Walking Street bars we have reviewed individually, the smaller scene in LK Metro, and the things that separate a good night from an expensive one.

If you want prices for the whole city rather than just GoGo bars, the [nightlife prices guide](/guides/pattaya-nightlife-prices/) covers beer bars, clubs and rooftops too. If you want the street itself, the [Walking Street guide](/guides/walking-street-guide/) has the sections, the timing and the safety notes. This page is about the bars.

## How a GoGo Bar Works

A GoGo bar is a bar with a stage. Dancers, usually in bikinis or a themed costume, rotate on and off the stage in shifts through the evening, with choreographed shows at intervals in the bigger venues. You sit, you drink, you watch. That is the whole of the required participation.

The sequence is the same in nearly every bar:

**The door.** A hostess or doorman greets you and waves you in. There is no cover charge at any of the bars on this page. If someone at a door asks for an entry fee, you are at a club or a ladyboy cabaret, not a GoGo bar.

**The seat.** You will be shown to a seat. Front row by the stage, a booth along the wall, or a stool at the bar. Front row costs the same as the back; the difference is how much attention you get.

**The first drink.** A waitress brings a menu and takes your order within a minute of sitting down. You are expected to buy at least one drink. Nurse it as long as you like; nobody will push you out.

**The dancers.** They come off stage between sets and some will come and sit with you, either because you have made eye contact or because the mamasan, the floor manager, has sent them. A dancer sitting with you will expect you to buy her a lady drink within a few minutes. If you do not want company, a polite "not right now, thank you" works and is not considered rude.

**Lady drinks.** A small drink, often a shot or a juice, that costs more than your beer. She gets a cut. This is how the dancers earn beyond their base salary and it is the core of the bar's business model. Buying one is a social gesture, not an obligation, and it does not commit you to anything else.

**Shows.** The bigger bars run production shows every hour or so: group routines, themed sets, sometimes acrobatics or fire. Smaller bars just rotate dancers. The shows are announced, the lights change, and the room turns to the stage.

**The bill.** Each drink comes with a chit in a cup on your table. When you want to leave you ask for the "check bin" and pay in cash or by card. Count the chits against the bill; mistakes happen more often in your disfavour than in your favour.

**Bar fines.** If you want a dancer to leave with you, you pay the bar a fee for taking her off shift. That is the bar fine. It goes to the bar; anything after that is between you and her. Nobody will raise the subject unless you do, and plenty of visitors spend a week in GoGo bars without ever paying one.

| Imported beer | 180–250 THB | 150–200 THB |
| Cocktail or spirit and mixer | 200–350 THB | 180–280 THB |
| Lady drink | 200–250 THB | 150–200 THB |
| Tip for a dancer on stage | 20 THB note | 20 THB note |
| Bar fine, standard bar | 600–1,000 THB | 500–800 THB |
| Bar fine, showcase bar | 1,000–2,000 THB | rarely applies |

Entry is free everywhere. Some bars, particularly the newer ones, quote a higher first-drink price than the menu suggests; ask before you order if the menu is not visible.

A realistic evening: two bars, four beers, two lady drinks, a couple of stage tips comes to about 1,500 THB. Three bars and a few more lady drinks gets you to 2,500 THB. A bar fine at a showcase venue on top of that, and you are at 4,000 THB before the night has really started, which is why people who know the street spend their drinking money in LK Metro and their bar-fine money on Walking Street.

## The Bars on Walking Street

Walking Street's GoGo bars cluster in three stretches: the entrance near Beach Road, the dense middle where the biggest names sit, and the far end towards the clubs and the pier. The side sois, Soi 15, Soi 16 and Soi Diamond, hold more. We have reviewed eleven individually; the links go to the full write-ups with current drink prices.

### Entrance and first stretch

[**Lighthouse A Go Go**](/nightlife/lighthouse-agogo/) is the first GoGo bar most people see, and that position suits it. It is mid-sized, clean, and organised around a single central stage, with none of the sensory overload further down the strip. If this is your first GoGo bar anywhere, start here. If it is your fiftieth, it makes a decent last stop on the way back to the taxi.

[**Fahrenheit A Go Go**](/nightlife/fahrenheit-agogo/) sits near the Soi Diamond intersection and runs multiple stages with rotating dancers and regular shows. Modern interior, professional staff, energetic without being aggressive. A good second bar.

### The middle stretch

This is where the foot traffic is heaviest and the big names compete.

[**Pin Up A Go Go**](/nightlife/pin-up-a-go-go/) is the bar most regulars will name first if you ask for the best on the street, and it has held that reputation for years. Spacious, polished, a large central stage, elevated VIP sections, and a standard of dancers and shows that justifies the crowd. Prices are at the top of the Walking Street range. Go once even if you go nowhere else.

[**Iron Club**](/nightlife/iron-club/) has been a fixture since 2009 and is one of the most recognisable bars on the strip. Larger than most of its neighbours, with a layout that gives nearly every seat a view. Strong entertainment, consistent crowd, first-timers and returners in roughly equal numbers.

[**Palace A Go Go**](/nightlife/palace-agogo/) holds its own in the busiest section by keeping it simple: a central stage, tiered seating with clear sightlines, fair prices and staff who know how to make a visitor comfortable. Less spectacle than Sapphire, less hype than Pin Up, and a lot of people's favourite for exactly that reason.

[**Windmill A Go Go**](/nightlife/windmill-agogo/) is a Walking Street institution operating as two separate venues, Windmill 1 and Windmill 2, under shared management. Windmill 1 is the older and more established. The name carries weight with returning visitors, and the bars have a reputation for being more uninhibited than most on the strip; if you are easily surprised, read the review first.

[**Sapphire A Go Go**](/nightlife/sapphire-agogo/) is the biggest room on the list and uses it: multiple stages, so there is always something happening wherever you sit, with headline production shows on the main stage. This is the spectacle option, and the one to pick if you want to see what a Walking Street GoGo bar looks like with the budget turned all the way up.

[**Shark A Go Go**](/nightlife/shark-agogo/) is not the biggest or the flashiest, and it does not try to be. What it does well is atmosphere: lively entertainment, genuinely friendly staff, and a room that feels more like a good night out than a transaction. A regulars' bar in the best sense.

[**Baccara A Go Go**](/nightlife/baccara-agogo/) is the legacy venue. It has been operating for decades on a street where bars open and close every season, and it still ranks near the top on every forum and review site. Dark, moody, well-sized, a central stage with good seating. If you know one Pattaya GoGo bar by name before you arrive, it is probably this one.

### The clubs end

[**Peppermint A Go Go**](/nightlife/peppermint-agogo/) sits at the far end near the beach and the mega-clubs, which means fewer walk-ins and a more settled crowd. Modern design, central stage, comfortable seating, and clearly a bar that invests in keeping itself fresh. Worth the walk past the crowded middle.

[**XS A Go Go**](/nightlife/xs-a-go-go/) is the newer-generation option: sharp LED lighting, a proper sound system, a nightclub feel and a crowd that skews younger. It opens at 21:00 rather than 20:00 and gets going later than the rest. If the traditional GoGo format feels dated to you, this is the bar that does not.

## LK Metro: The Other GoGo Scene

Fifteen minutes' walk inland from Walking Street, tucked between [Soi Buakhao](/areas/soi-buakhao/) and Soi Diana, LK Metro is a compact grid of thirty or forty small bars that functions as a miniature Walking Street without the tour groups. The GoGo bars here are smaller, the stages are closer, the drinks are 20 to 30 percent cheaper, and the crowd is expats and long-stay regulars rather than first-night visitors.

Champagne and Kiss are the two most often named, with Crystal Club and Showgirls among the other established rooms. None of them runs a production show; they rotate dancers, keep the lights low and the music at a level where you can talk. If you are in Pattaya for more than a few nights, you will probably end up drinking here more than on Walking Street, and your wallet will thank you.

We have not yet reviewed the LK Metro bars individually; that is on the list. The [Soi Buakhao area guide](/areas/soi-buakhao/) covers the neighbourhood.

## Soi 6 Is Not a GoGo Street

Visitors often lump [Soi 6](/areas/soi-6/) in with the GoGo scene. It is a different thing. Soi 6 is a street of small open-fronted beer bars, most with rooms upstairs, where the arrangement is short-time and direct and there is no stage. It is busiest in the afternoon and early evening, it is cheaper, and it has its own etiquette. If you are looking for dancers and shows, it is not the place. If you are looking for Soi 6, our [area guide](/areas/soi-6/) and the [venue reviews](/nightlife/) cover it bar by bar.

## Etiquette and Mistakes

Most of the friction visitors run into at GoGo bars comes from not knowing five or six unwritten rules.

- **No photos.** Not of the stage, not of the dancers, not of the room. Every bar enforces this and the enforcement is fast. Phone away, or face down on the table.
- **Do not touch a dancer on stage.** Tipping is fine: fold a 20 THB note and hold it up, or tuck it into a garter if she offers. Grabbing is not, and the security staff are large.
- **Count your chits.** Every drink adds a slip to the cup on your table. Before you pay, check the number of slips against the bill. Bill padding is uncommon at the bars on this page but not unheard of on the street.
- **Say no clearly and early.** A dancer who sits down uninvited will expect a lady drink. "Not right now, thank you" is fine. Letting her sit for ten minutes and then refusing is where the friction starts.
- **Know the difference between a GoGo bar and a coyote bar.** Coyote bars have dancers on a stage but they are not available for bar fines; it is a dance show, not a GoGo bar. Some venues on Soi Diamond and Second Road operate this way and are not always clear about it.
- **Know which bars are ladyboy bars.** A handful of Walking Street GoGo bars are staffed entirely by ladyboys and say so at the door. None of the eleven bars above are among them. If it matters to you, look at the sign before you sit down; our [ladyboys in Pattaya guide](/guides/ladyboys-pattaya/) lists where those bars are and covers Tiffany's and Alcazar.
- **Do not arrive drunk.** The bars are dark, the drinks are strong, and the walk back to the hotel is long. Arriving sober and leaving merry is the right way round.
- **Do not argue with the mamasan.** She runs the floor, she decides who sits where, and she has heard every argument. If there is a problem with a bill, ask for the manager calmly; if there is a problem with a bar fine, walk away and choose another bar.

## When to Go

GoGo bars open around 20:00 and are empty until 21:00. The dancers arrive, the lights come up, and the first shows start around 21:30. Peak is 22:30 to 00:30. After 01:00 the crowd thins, the dancers who are still on stage are the ones who have not been bar-fined, and the energy drops. Closing is 03:00 by the sign and often earlier in practice.

Weekends are busier, louder and slightly more expensive in the sense that the good seats go fast. Weeknights are relaxed and the staff have more time. High season, November to February, means full bars every night; low season, June to September, means the same bars half-empty and dancers who are glad to see you.

## Safety

Pattaya's GoGo bars are not dangerous. The bars on this page are established businesses that depend on repeat custom and behave accordingly. The risks are the ordinary ones of a night out in a strange city.

Keep your phone and wallet in a front pocket or a zipped bag; the street outside is crowded and the crowd is not all tourists. Drink at a pace you can manage; Thai beer is 5 to 6 percent and served fast. Do not leave a drink unattended. Do not buy, carry or accept anything illegal; the police run checkpoints on the roads out of Walking Street and the penalties are severe. Agree the fare before getting into a baht bus or a motorbike taxi at 2am.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there an entry fee at Pattaya GoGo bars?

No. None of the GoGo bars on Walking Street or in LK Metro charge a cover. You are expected to buy at least one drink.

### What is a bar fine?

A fee paid to the bar if you want a dancer to leave with you before the end of her shift, typically 600 to 1,000 THB at standard Walking Street GoGo bars and 1,000 to 2,000 THB at the showcase venues. It compensates the bar; any arrangement beyond that is between you and her.

### Can I go to a GoGo bar alone?

Yes, and most visitors do. Solo visitors are the norm and get seated and served the same as groups.

### Can couples or women visit GoGo bars?

Yes. Couples are common on Walking Street and the bars are used to them. Women visiting without a partner are less common but not unwelcome; the bigger bars with production shows, Sapphire and Pin Up in particular, see mixed groups every night.

### Is there a dress code?

No. Shorts, T-shirt and sandals are fine everywhere on this page. The nightclubs at the far end of Walking Street are stricter; the GoGo bars are not.

### Can I take photos inside?

No. This is enforced at every bar. Phones face down.

### How many bars can I do in a night?

Two or three comfortably. Each bar is worth 45 minutes to an hour if you are watching the shows; more if you are enjoying the company. Trying to see eight bars in a night is how first-timers end up spending 5,000 THB and remembering none of it.

### Are the dancers Thai?

Mostly, with a share from neighbouring countries in some bars. Most are from Isaan in the north-east. English varies from fluent to very limited; a smile and simple sentences go a long way.

## Final Thoughts

Pattaya's GoGo bars are exactly what they look like and less intimidating than they sound. Start at Lighthouse or Fahrenheit, see Pin Up or Sapphire once for the full production, find the smaller bar that suits you, and then discover LK Metro for the nights when you want the same thing at two-thirds of the price. Keep your phone in your pocket, count your chits, and say no politely. Do that and the bars will treat you well, because treating visitors well is how they have stayed in business on that street for forty years.

The eleven reviews linked above go into each bar in detail, with current drink prices. Start with whichever one sounds most like your kind of night.
