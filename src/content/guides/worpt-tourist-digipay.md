Thailand is testing a government sandbox that lets foreign tourists spend stablecoins without a Thai bank account. The live app in that programme is called **WORPT**. You convert USDT or USDC into baht, then pay merchants through the ordinary PromptPay QR network — the shop receives baht, not crypto.

This is a **pilot**, not a finished national payment system. Fees, limits, supported coins and where it is accepted can change during the sandbox. We have not tested the app ourselves. Last checked **13 November 2026**.

## What Tourist DigiPay and WORPT Are

Tourist DigiPay is a regulatory sandbox. In the coverage of this launch, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and a Bank of Thailand-licensed e-money firm are the regulators in the picture. WORPT is the consumer app that has gone live inside that sandbox.

It is aimed at **foreign tourists**, and it is not yet available to Thai users.

The design is deliberate: crypto is converted into baht before it reaches the merchant. That works around Thailand's existing ban on using crypto directly as payment for goods and services, rather than lifting the ban.

## Who Is Behind It

WORPT is a collaboration between:

- **Waan Exchange Co., Ltd. (Waanx)** — a digital-asset exchange. The SEC approved Waanx to join the Tourist DigiPay Sandbox on **25 September 2026**. That date is the sandbox approval, not the date of Waanx's underlying exchange licence.
- **Wipay Co., Ltd.** (also written WiPay) — an e-money provider licensed by the Bank of Thailand. Wipay also operates a separate tourist wallet, Wi Wallet, which is a different product.
- **Tether**, the issuer of USDT, is described as a partner in the launch coverage.

## How It Works

According to the companies and the reporting around the launch:

1. Download the WORPT app on **iOS or Android**.
2. Verify your identity with e-KYC: a **face scan** and a read of the **chip on an electronic passport (e-passport)**.
3. Link an external crypto wallet and transfer supported stablecoins. Coverage of the launch names **USDT and USDC**. Other coins such as Bitcoin are not listed in those reports; whether they are accepted has not been confirmed in the sources we checked.
4. The system runs transaction checks (KYT) on the incoming wallet, then converts the coins into baht in an e-wallet.
5. Pay by scanning a **Thai QR / PromptPay** code. You do not need a Thai bank account. The merchant is paid in baht and, according to the companies, does not need to change how they accept QR payments.

## Fees

Wipay has said the currency conversion fee is **initially 1.5%**. That figure comes from a mid-November 2026 launch interview; earlier coverage of the app had not yet published a fee. Treat it as the starting rate they announced, not a guarantee.

Limits, network fees for the incoming transfer, and any other charges were not clearly published in the same reports.

## Where You Can Spend It — and What We Know About Pattaya

**The Mall Group** is the first department-store partner to accept WORPT payments, according to both The Mall Group and launch coverage. Tourist privileges tied to the WORPT, THIM and Wi Wallet apps have been described for **Paragon Department Store** and **EM DISTRICT** in Bangkok.

The companies also describe payment as standard Thai QR / PromptPay, with merchants receiving baht as usual and needing no special setup. In principle that sounds broader than a single mall group. In practice, the only named retail partner in the coverage we checked is The Mall Group.

**Pattaya is not mentioned** in those reports. Marketing has been described as starting at Suvarnabhumi Airport, then expanding to other international airports such as Phuket and Koh Samui. We cannot say from the sources whether WORPT is usable day to day in Pattaya. Until that is confirmed, do not rely on it as your only way to pay here.

## Visa Card Plans

As of late October 2026, a **physical Visa prepaid card** was planned for mid-November 2026, and a **virtual card** for early 2027, so the same baht balance could be spent in shops and online. A mid-November Mall Group announcement also refers to spending via Thai QR or a Visa prepaid card, without stating that the physical card had already been issued. We have not seen a later confirmation that the card is in users' hands. That may have changed since — check the app.

## How It Compares With Other Ways to Spend Crypto in Pattaya

WORPT is one new official route, not a replacement for the options already used in Pattaya:

- **OTC cash offices** still matter if you need baht in hand. Spreads are typically 3–4%. See our [crypto to Thai Baht guide](/guides/crypto-to-thb-pattaya/).
- **Crypto-funded Visa cards** already let you pay at card terminals from a USDT balance, often around 1.5% on card payments. They are not a Thai-licensed sandbox product. Details are in the same crypto guide.
- **Wise, TrueMoney and similar apps** cover PromptPay from ordinary currency, not from USDT. See [Thai banking and QR payments](/guides/thai-banking-qr-payments/).

If you are visiting Pattaya, treat WORPT as an extra option to look at, not as something you can already count on at a 7-Eleven or a beach bar.

## FAQ

### Can I pay with USDT in Thailand without a Thai bank account?

That is the point of the Tourist DigiPay sandbox and the WORPT app, as described by the companies: convert USDT or USDC to baht and pay via PromptPay QR, without a Thai bank account. It is still a pilot.

### Does the WORPT app work in Pattaya?

The sources we checked do not mention Pattaya. The first named retail partner is The Mall Group in Bangkok. Payment is described as ordinary PromptPay QR, but acceptance outside that listed partner has not been confirmed.

### Can I deposit Bitcoin or other coins?

Launch coverage names **USDT and USDC** as the supported stablecoins. Bitcoin and other coins are not listed there. We have not seen an official statement that they are rejected — only that they are not named.

### Do I need an e-passport?

Identity checks are described as a face scan plus reading the chip on an electronic passport. If your passport has no chip, this flow as described would not work.

### Is this a recommendation to use WORPT?

No. This page explains a government-sandbox product from published reports. It is not a recommendation to use the app or to hold any stablecoin. Check the app, the operators and the official terms before you send any funds. Details can change during the pilot.
