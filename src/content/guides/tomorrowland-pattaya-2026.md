Tomorrowland Thailand sold out. All three days, in under an hour, back in March.

So if you are reading this in September, you are in one of two situations. Either you hold a pass and now need to solve accommodation and transport in a city that has never absorbed a crowd like this. Or you do not hold one, and you want to know whether there is still a legitimate way in.

This guide covers both. It is written from Pattaya and updated as the organisers release details. Last checked: **3 September 2026**.

This is the first Tomorrowland edition staged anywhere in Thailand, and the largest music event the country has hosted. The CORE and Freedom stages are coming to Asia for the first time.

## Tickets: Where Things Actually Stand

Pre-registration opened on 8 January 2026. The worldwide sale went live on 7 March at 16:00 GMT+7 and the entire allocation was gone within the hour.

Face value at that sale:

| Pass | Price |
|---|---|
| Day ticket | 5,100 THB |
| Full Madness Pass (all three days) | 12,500 THB |
| Full Madness Comfort Pass (VIP) | 20,200 THB |

**One rule to check before anything else: the minimum age is 20, not 18**, and it goes by your birthday rather than your birth year. Mixed-age group, check every passport now.

Those were the original prices, not what you will pay now. Treat them as the benchmark for judging whether an offer you find is reasonable or predatory.

### The two legitimate routes left

**The official waiting list.** You register inside your account on tomorrowland.com. When a buyer surrenders a pass, it is offered on to the list at original face value. This is slow and there is no guarantee, but it is free and it is real.

**The official Ticket Exchange.** Tomorrowland runs its own resale desk. Passes sold through it transfer properly and stay valid.

**Global Journey packages** bundle travel with entry, cost more, and historically stay available after standalone tickets are gone. If you are still hunting in September, this is the route most likely to actually work.

### What not to do

Do not buy from third-party resale sites. Tomorrowland passes are personalised, and the organisation cancels tickets that change hands outside its own exchange. You find out at the gate, after the flight and the hotel are already paid for. If a listing does not live on tomorrowland.com, assume the money is gone.

We have no affiliate relationship with any ticket seller and we are not going to send you to one.

## Wisdom Valley: Where It Actually Is

This is the detail most people get wrong when they book.

The address is 188/5 Moo 1, Tambon Khao Mai Kaeo, Bang Lamung district, Chonburi — Plus Code **X22C+V93**, which you can paste straight into Google Maps.

That puts it inland, roughly due east of the coast. It is not on the beach and it is not north of the city, which several guides claim. The beach hotels everyone is booking are a 30-minute drive away on an ordinary Tuesday, and 11 to 13 December will not be an ordinary Tuesday. Every routing decision below follows from that one fact.

The site itself covers 237 acres of hills and lakes, which is what allows the full Tomorrowland production to land here rather than a scaled-down touring version.

What is still unpublished as of early September: the shuttle route map, gate locations, and set times. Those are the pieces that will determine which neighbourhoods are genuinely convenient. We will update this section the day they appear.

## Where to Stay

The official Tomorrowland hotel packages — the ones that bundled shuttle transport to the gate — sold out on 28 February 2026, before the ticket sale itself. If you did not get one, you are booking independently and arranging your own way to the site.

Book refundable rates now and adjust later. Pattaya has a lot of rooms, but it has never had 50,000 extra people arriving on one weekend.

We measured every neighbourhood against the site, and the result is not what the booking portals tell you: the whole coastal strip sits within a four-kilometre band, and **Na Jomtien is the furthest area from the venue, not the closest**. The full breakdown with our picks per area is in [where to stay by distance to Wisdom Valley](/guides/tomorrowland-hotels-wisdom-valley/).

The short version:

**[Jomtien](/areas/jomtien/)** is the sensible middle. A wide range of hotels from guesthouses to beachfront properties, a proper beach, and it keeps you out of the central-Pattaya traffic knot that will be at its worst that weekend.

**[Pratumnak](/areas/pratumnak/)** sits on the hill between South Pattaya and Jomtien. Quieter, more upscale, quick access in both directions. Good if you want to sleep properly between festival days.

**Central Pattaya and [Walking Street](/areas/walking-street/)** are the best base for after-parties and the worst base for getting out to the venue. Pick this if the city is as much of the trip as the festival.

**[East Pattaya](/areas/east-pattaya/) and [Soi Buakhao](/areas/soi-buakhao/)** are the cheapest rooms in the city, and for once the geography helps — they are inland, which is the direction you need to travel. Rooms that go for 500 THB in a normal week will not stay at 500 THB in December.

## Getting to Pattaya

**Suvarnabhumi (BKK)** is the main international gateway, about 120 km from Pattaya via Motorway 7. Airport buses, private transfers and Grab all run this route.

**Don Mueang (DMK)** handles most budget carriers and has no direct link — you connect through Bangkok first. If you have a choice between the two on price alone, factor the extra transfer in before deciding.

**U-Tapao (UTP)** is roughly 40 km south of Pattaya and handles a growing number of regional flights. If a routing through U-Tapao exists for your origin, it is the shortest transfer by a wide margin. Check it before defaulting to Bangkok.

Expect the organisers to announce dedicated airport shuttles closer to the date — previous international editions have all had them.

One thing worth knowing now: **10 December is a Thai public holiday and 11 December is a bridge day**, so the Bangkok–Pattaya road carries a domestic long weekend on top of the festival. Be in Pattaya by Wednesday the 9th. Full airport comparison, the U-Tapao case and the last leg to the site: [getting to Tomorrowland Thailand](/guides/tomorrowland-getting-there/). ## December Weather

Mid-December is the best weather window Pattaya gets, which is presumably why the date was chosen.

Days run warm rather than punishing, nights cool off enough to be comfortable, humidity is at its annual low and the monsoon is finished. A brief shower is possible but not typical. Sea breezes keep evenings on the coast fresh.

The practical consequence: you are packing for heat during the day and for a genuinely cool night at an outdoor venue on a hill. Bring one layer.

## What to Pack

Short version below. The [full packing guide](/guides/tomorrowland-packing-guide/) covers what December nights actually feel like inland, the cashless site, and the insurance exclusions worth reading before you fly.

- Lightweight, breathable clothing — cotton or moisture-wicking
- One warm layer for the night stages
- Closed shoes with grip, not sandals
- A compact rain poncho (umbrellas are useless in a crowd)
- High-SPF sunscreen and a hat for the daytime stages
- Refillable water bottle
- Earplugs — this is a full Tomorrowland sound system
- Portable charger
- A crossbody bag or waist pack you can dance in
- Cash as well as cards, and a photocopy of your passport with the original left in the hotel safe

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tomorrowland Thailand sold out?

Yes. The full allocation sold within an hour of the worldwide sale on 7 March 2026. The official waiting list and the official Ticket Exchange are the only legitimate routes remaining.

### Can I still buy a ticket somewhere?

Only through Tomorrowland's own channels — the waiting list in your account, the official Ticket Exchange, or a Global Journey package. Passes bought on third-party resale sites are cancelled.

### Where exactly is the venue?

Wisdom Valley, at Khao Mai Kaew in Bang Lamung district, Chonburi. It is inland, roughly 30 minutes from central Pattaya, not a beachfront site.

### How do I get from my hotel to the festival?

Shuttle routes have not been published yet. Official hotel packages included transport but sold out in February. Grab operates throughout Pattaya and will surge hard at closing time each night. We will update this page when the routes are confirmed.

### What is the weather like in December?

The best of the year — warm dry days, cool nights, almost no rain. Bring a layer for the evening.

### Is Pattaya safe?

Yes, with the usual precautions for any large tourist city: watch your belongings, use Grab late at night, and stay entirely clear of drugs. Thai penalties are severe and a festival wristband changes nothing. Our [first-time visitor guide](/guides/first-time-pattaya/) covers the rest.
