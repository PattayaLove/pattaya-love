Every ThaiFriendly review I found before signing up was written by someone who had clearly never used it, padded out with the member count from the homepage and a screenshot of the pricing page. So here is a different kind of review: I lived in Pattaya, I kept a ThaiFriendly account for a month in 2026, I used the free tier and the 7-day Premium trial, and this is what actually happened.

One thing up front, because it matters for how much weight you give the rest: I have not yet met anyone from the app in person. This review covers the app itself — the profiles, the messaging, the Premium trial, who is on the other end. When the first meeting happens, this page gets updated. ## The Short Verdict

**Worth it if** you are in Pattaya (or coming) and you want to talk to a lot of Thai women quickly, whether you are looking for a holiday companion, a date, or a girlfriend. Nothing else comes close on volume, and the women reply.

**Not worth it if** you want a relationship-first pool with fewer freelancers, in which case [ThaiCupid](/guides/thaicupid-review/) is the better fit, or if you refuse to pay anything, because the free tier is too limited to be useful for more than a look around.

**Do this:** sign up through the app, verify your photo, and take the 7-day free Premium trial. That week tells you everything you need to know for your own situation, and it costs nothing if you set a reminder to cancel.

## What ThaiFriendly Is

<!-- TODO: swap for affiliate link once the ThaiFriendly affiliate account is approved -->
<a href="https://www.thaifriendly.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">ThaiFriendly</a> is a Thai dating site that launched in 2010 and now claims close to two million members, with about a thousand new profiles a day. It is available as a website and as an app for iOS and Android, and it is by a wide margin the most used dating platform in Pattaya.

It works like an old-fashioned dating site rather than a swipe app. There is no matching. You set filters for location and age, you see who is online, and you message anyone you like. Anyone can message you. That single design choice is why it works so differently from Tinder, and why the experience for a foreign man in Pattaya is so far from what he is used to at home.

## Signing Up and Verification

Signing up takes two minutes: email, a photo, age, location, a line about yourself. I used the app rather than the website, and I would recommend that, for a reason I will get to in the Premium section.

Verify your photo as soon as the profile exists. ThaiFriendly puts a verified badge on profiles that have passed its photo check, and the women on the site use it as a filter. An unverified profile of a foreign man in Pattaya looks like every scam and every married-man-on-holiday account they have learned to ignore. My response rate was noticeably better once the badge was there, and it costs nothing.

Fill in the profile properly. Not a novel, but a real photo of your face, a real sentence about why you are in Thailand, and an honest line about what you are looking for. The women reading it are working through a lot of profiles and they filter fast.

## The Free Account

The free tier lets you browse, see who is online, receive messages and send them, with restrictions on how often you can send. It is enough to confirm that the app is real and that the women in your area are real, and not much more.

The wall you hit is speed. On ThaiFriendly the conversations move fast, several at once, and the free account's limits on sending mean you are constantly waiting while the other side has already moved on to the next message in her inbox. You also cannot see who has viewed your profile, which is the single most useful signal on the site for deciding who to write to.

I have not pinned down the exact current cooldown between free messages; it has changed over the years and I will update this section when I have tested it again properly. The practical point does not depend on the exact number: free is for looking, Premium is for using.

## The 7-Day Free Premium Trial

This is the part almost no review mentions, and it is the reason to sign up through the app rather than the website.

ThaiFriendly offers a **7-day free Premium trial through the mobile app**. It appears as an in-app offer after you have set up your profile. Accept it and Premium unlocks immediately: unlimited messaging with no cooldown, a list of who has viewed your profile, higher placement in the search results the women are browsing, no ads, and full-size photos.

Two warnings. First, it is a trial in the usual app-store sense: it converts to a paid monthly subscription when the seven days end unless you cancel. Set a calendar reminder for day six, because the monthly price is the most expensive way to buy Premium. Second, the offer is tied to the app, so if you only ever use the website you may never see it.

Used properly, the trial is a free week of the full product. For a two-week holiday that is half your trip covered.

## What Happened When Premium Was On

The difference was immediate. With Premium active and the verified badge on the profile, I had more women writing to me first in a single evening than I have had in months on Tinder or Bumble in Germany. Not likes, not matches sitting in a queue — actual messages, opening conversations.

The reply rate on messages I sent was high as well. Most women I wrote to answered. That is not because my profile was exceptional; it is how the platform works. The women on ThaiFriendly are there to talk to foreign men, the interface makes it easy, and the cultural default in Thailand is to be friendly and curious rather than guarded. If you have spent years on Western apps being ignored unless you look like a model, the first evening on ThaiFriendly is genuinely disorienting.

The conversations move to LINE fast. Two or three messages, then a LINE ID. That is normal in Thailand and not by itself a warning sign; LINE is simply where Thais talk. The warning signs are what comes in the first LINE messages, which brings us to the part of this review you actually came for.

## Who Is on ThaiFriendly in Pattaya

I set my filters to Pattaya only and 18 to 24 years old. That is the youngest bracket and the city with the heaviest nightlife, so read what follows with that in mind. A search for 28 to 35 in Chiang Mai would show a very different population.

With those filters the number of active profiles was enormous. Hundreds online on any given evening, and the women in the photos were, to be blunt about it, a very attractive group. That part of ThaiFriendly's reputation is accurate.

What is also accurate is the freelancer share. Of the conversations I had, roughly **six out of ten** turned to money within the first handful of messages. Sometimes it was a question about my hotel and how long I was staying, followed by a price. Sometimes the price came first. The women were direct about it, which I would rather have than the alternative. Rates were quoted openly, in baht, for short time or for the night, and in a couple of cases for a full week as a travel companion.

The other four in ten were harder to categorise from chat alone. Students, women working in shops and hotels, a few who said they were just bored and practising English. Some of them may have been freelancers taking a slower approach; some were clearly not. Without meeting anyone I cannot say how that four splits, and I am not going to guess.

The signals that separated the two groups were the ones listed in the [main guide](/guides/dating-apps-thailand/#the-pattaya-reality-freelancers-and-how-to-tell): questions about the hotel and length of stay in the first two messages, online at 2am, photos taken in hotel rooms, and the word "sponsor" anywhere in the profile. In practice, once you have read twenty profiles you can call it from the profile alone most of the time.

If you are looking for a paid companion, ThaiFriendly in Pattaya is the most efficient tool for that in existence, and the women are more upfront about terms than anyone in a bar. If you are looking for something else, you need to filter hard, and a different age bracket or a daytime-only approach would cut the freelancer share considerably.

## What Premium Costs

After the trial, ThaiFriendly Premium is priced like most dating subscriptions: expensive by the month, reasonable by the year.

- **1 month:** around $25
- **3 months:** around $50–60
- **12 months:** around $100–120, which is $8–10 a month

Payment goes through the app store if you subscribe in the app, or by card on the website. The website price is sometimes slightly lower because it avoids the app-store cut; worth comparing before you commit to a year.

For a holiday, take the trial and, if you want more, one month. For anyone spending months a year in Thailand the annual plan is the obvious choice. For anyone in between, three months covers a long winter stay.

## ThaiFriendly vs Tinder and Bumble at Home

The honest comparison is not between ThaiFriendly and other Thai apps; it is between ThaiFriendly and the apps most of us have been using for years.

On Tinder or Bumble in Germany, Britain or the United States, an average-looking man in his thirties or forties gets a handful of matches a week, most of which never turn into a conversation, and is expected to do all the work. On ThaiFriendly in Pattaya the same man gets a full inbox, women who write first, and conversations that go somewhere the same day. The women are friendly, interested, and quick to reply.

Part of that is the platform design, part is Thai culture, and part, in Pattaya, is that a large share of the women have a commercial interest in talking to you. All three are true at once. The mistake visitors make is to credit the first two and ignore the third, and then feel cheated a week later. Go in knowing all three and ThaiFriendly is exactly what it appears to be.

## Safety

ThaiFriendly itself is a legitimate, long-running business. The risks are in individual profiles, and they are the same ones that apply to every dating app in Thailand: romance scams that end with a sick relative and a request for money, crypto "investment" profiles run from scam compounds across the border, stolen photos, and, in Pattaya, the small number of freelancers who work with a partner or wait until you are asleep.

The rules that cover all of it: never send money to anyone you have not met, do a short video call before meeting, meet in public the first time, block anyone who mentions crypto or forex, and keep your valuables in the hotel safe. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a ThaiFriendly free trial?

Yes. A 7-day free Premium trial is offered through the mobile app after you set up a profile. It converts to a paid monthly subscription unless you cancel before the seven days end, so set a reminder.

### How much does ThaiFriendly Premium cost?

Around $25 for one month, $50–60 for three months, and $100–120 for twelve months. The annual plan works out to $8–10 a month.

### Is ThaiFriendly legit?

The company and the platform are legitimate and have operated since 2010. As on every dating app, individual profiles range from genuine to scam. Verify your own photo, filter for verified profiles, never send money, and video-call before meeting.

### Can I use ThaiFriendly for free?

Yes, with limits on how often you can send messages and without seeing who viewed your profile. It is enough to browse and confirm the app is real; for actual use, the free trial or a paid month makes a large difference.

### Should I verify my ThaiFriendly profile?

Yes. The verified badge is free, takes a few minutes, and Thai women on the site use it to filter out fake and married-man accounts. My response rate improved noticeably after verifying.

### Are most women on ThaiFriendly in Pattaya freelancers?

With filters set to Pattaya and ages 18 to 24, roughly six in ten of my conversations turned to money within a few messages. A different age range or city would give a different ratio. The profile signals that identify freelancers are covered in the [main dating apps guide](/guides/dating-apps-thailand/).

### How do I cancel ThaiFriendly Premium?

If you subscribed through the app, cancel in your Apple or Google subscription settings, not in ThaiFriendly itself. If you subscribed on the website, cancel in your ThaiFriendly account settings. Either way, do it before the renewal date; there is no partial refund.

## Verdict

ThaiFriendly does exactly what it says. It puts you in front of a very large number of Thai women who will actually talk to you, and in Pattaya it does that faster than anything else. The free tier is a demo. Premium is the product, and the 7-day trial through the app means you can find out for yourself without spending a baht.

Just go in with your eyes open about who is on the other end. In Pattaya, with the filters most visitors use, the majority of those conversations will turn to money. Whether that is a feature or a problem is entirely up to you, and ThaiFriendly will not decide it for you.
