ThaiCupid is the app people recommend when you tell them you want something more than a holiday romance in Thailand. It is older than ThaiFriendly, bigger by its own count, owned by a listed Australian company, and priced like it knows you are serious. This review explains what you get for the money, who is actually on the other end, and whether it makes sense for someone whose Thailand is mostly Pattaya.

A disclosure before anything else: I have not held a ThaiCupid account. I spent a month on ThaiFriendly in Pattaya, which is written up in the [ThaiFriendly review](/guides/thaifriendly-review/), and I use that month as the comparison throughout. Everything about ThaiCupid here comes from Cupid Media's own published terms and pricing, the app's documentation, and a careful read of what long-term users consistently report. When I write about how ThaiCupid feels to use, that is their experience, not mine, and I say so. If that is not the review you want, the [dating apps in Thailand guide](/guides/dating-apps-thailand/) covers the whole field in less depth.

## The Short Verdict

**Worth it if** you are looking for a Thai girlfriend or wife rather than company for the week, you are prepared to pay for Gold at minimum, and you are willing to start conversations a few weeks before you fly rather than the night you land.

**Not worth it if** you want to open an app in Pattaya at 9pm and have plans by 10. ThaiCupid is slow by design. It is also not worth it on the free tier, which lets you look but not talk.

**Compared with ThaiFriendly:** fewer women, older women, far fewer freelancers, and a fee before you can send a single message. Whether that is better depends entirely on what you came for.

## What ThaiCupid Is

<!-- TODO: swap for Cupid Media affiliate link once approved -->
<a href="https://www.thaicupid.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">ThaiCupid</a> launched in 2002 and belongs to Cupid Media, an Australian company that runs a network of nationality- and interest-based dating sites: FilipinoCupid, VietnamCupid, ChristianCupid and a few dozen more. ThaiCupid claims over three million members. It is available as a website in more than twenty languages, including Thai, and as an app for iOS and Android.

The company's own positioning is unambiguous. Success stories on the site are wedding photos. The profile setup asks about religion, whether you have or want children, and whether you would relocate. This is a matchmaking site with a modern interface, not a chat app with a dating skin.

## How It Differs From ThaiFriendly

The two apps get lumped together because both connect foreign men with Thai women. In practice they are built on opposite principles.

**Who can talk to whom.** On ThaiFriendly, anyone can message anyone, free or paid. On ThaiCupid, a free member can create a profile, browse, and send an "interest" (a like), but can only exchange messages with members who pay. Since the great majority of Thai women on the site are on free accounts, that means a foreign man on a free account can effectively talk to nobody. The design forces one side to pay, and it is not the Thai side.

**Who writes first.** On ThaiFriendly in Pattaya, women writing first is the norm; in my month there I had more unsolicited opening messages than I could answer. On ThaiCupid the consistent report from users is the reverse: Thai women rarely open a conversation, and a woman who does message an average-looking man first is treated as a warning sign rather than a welcome. Partly that is culture, partly it is that the women who would do that are on ThaiFriendly instead.

**Profile depth.** ThaiFriendly profiles are a photo and two lines. ThaiCupid profiles run to a page: appearance, lifestyle, background, religion, what she wants in a partner, sometimes an essay. That slows browsing down and filters for people willing to put in effort on both sides.

**Age.** ThaiFriendly in Pattaya skews to the early twenties. ThaiCupid's centre of gravity, by every account, is late twenties to forties on the women's side and forties to sixties on the men's.

**Location.** ThaiFriendly's Pattaya pool is enormous. ThaiCupid's is real but small, and many of the women who list Pattaya or Chonburi live in Sattahip, Rayong or Bang Saen and come into the city occasionally. A lot of ThaiCupid's members are in Bangkok, Isaan and the north.

| Rank above other members in search | No | No | Yes |
| Message translation (Thai to English) | No | No | Yes |
| Advanced matching, VIP profile highlight | No | No | Yes |

The one Platinum feature that earns its price is **message translation**. A large share of women on ThaiCupid write in Thai or in an English that Google Translate struggles with, and the built-in translation runs both ways inside the chat. If you are going to have long conversations with someone whose English is limited, which on ThaiCupid you will, Platinum pays for itself in the first week of not copying and pasting.

Ranking higher in search matters less than the sales page suggests. Thai women on ThaiCupid are not scrolling through hundreds of foreign men a night the way men scroll through women; they respond to messages, and a message from a Gold member lands the same as one from Platinum.

## What ThaiCupid Costs

Prices vary slightly by country, currency and whether you buy on the website or through an app store, so treat these as close rather than exact:

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | around $30 | around $60 ($20/month) | around $120 ($10/month) |
| **Platinum** | around $35 | around $75 ($25/month) | around $150 ($12.50/month) |

Cupid Media discounts steeply for commitment and is not shy about it; the 12-month Platinum plan costs about four monthly Gold payments. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel in your account settings before the renewal date, and the refund policy is strict, so buy the length you will actually use.

Against ThaiFriendly this is roughly 20 to 40 percent more at every tier, and ThaiFriendly has a free week through its app that ThaiCupid does not match. For a two-week visit, one month of Gold is the sensible purchase. For anyone genuinely looking for a partner and prepared to spend three to six months on it, which is the realistic timeline users describe, the annual Platinum plan is the right buy.

## Who Is Actually on ThaiCupid

This is where ThaiCupid earns its reputation, and where I am relying on other people's accounts rather than my own.

The women are, broadly, older than on ThaiFriendly, more likely to have a job they will name, more likely to have children from a previous relationship, and more likely to say directly that they are looking for a husband. Many are from Isaan and the north and have watched a sister or cousin marry a foreigner. Many are divorced. English levels range from fluent to nearly none, which is what the translation feature is for.

The freelancer share is much lower than ThaiFriendly's. It is not zero; some bar and massage workers keep a ThaiCupid profile as a longer-term play. But ThaiCupid's paywall, its long profiles and its slow pace make it a poor tool for someone who wants to be paid tonight, and most of those women simply are not there. In my month on ThaiFriendly with filters set to Pattaya and 18 to 24, roughly six in ten conversations turned to money within a few messages. Every account of ThaiCupid I have read puts the equivalent figure far lower, and the accounts that quantify it land somewhere between one in five and one in ten.

The men are older too, mostly forties to sixties, mostly from the United States, Australia, Britain and Northern Europe, and mostly paying. That last point matters: because men pay to message, the women get fewer messages than on ThaiFriendly and answer a higher share of them. Users describe response rates around a third to a half for a decent profile with a personal first message, which is lower than ThaiFriendly's near-universal replies and much higher than Tinder anywhere.

## The Pattaya Angle

If your Thailand is Pattaya, ThaiCupid is a preparation tool, not a nightlife tool.

The Pattaya pool is small and the women in it are not looking for a drink at midnight. What works, according to users who have done it, is starting two to four weeks before you fly: set your location to Pattaya or Chonburi, message a dozen women whose profiles you actually read, video-call the two or three who reply properly, and arrive with lunch already arranged. That is a completely different rhythm from ThaiFriendly, where the same result takes an hour on your first evening, and it produces a completely different kind of meeting.

For the daytime dates that ThaiCupid generates, the usual Pattaya advice applies: Terminal 21 or Central Pattaya for a first coffee, a Thai restaurant rather than a steakhouse, and a [guest-friendly hotel](/guides/guest-friendly-hotels-pattaya/) without a joiner fee if things go well. ## Scams and Safety

ThaiCupid has fewer scammers than ThaiFriendly for the same reason it has fewer freelancers: the paywall and the effort involved. It does not have none.

The ThaiCupid-specific pattern is the long romance scam. Because the site attracts men who want a relationship and are willing to wait, it attracts scammers who are willing to wait too. Weeks of warm, well-written messages, sometimes with translated Thai for authenticity, then a sick parent, a lost job, a visa fee, a plane ticket she needs help with. The amounts are larger than ThaiFriendly's because the trust is deeper.

The rules are the same ones from the [main guide's safety section](/guides/dating-apps-thailand/#scams-and-safety), with two ThaiCupid-specific additions:

- **Video-call within the first two weeks.** On ThaiCupid a woman who refuses a video call after two weeks of daily chat is hiding something. Shyness is a real Thai trait, but it does not last a fortnight with someone she is telling she loves.
- **Be suspicious of first messages.** The ThaiFriendly instinct, where a woman writing first is normal and welcome, is the wrong one here. On ThaiCupid an unsolicited opener from a much younger, model-quality profile is the most common start to a scam.

ThaiCupid does verify profiles, and its verified badge means a government ID was checked. Filter for it.

## Verdict

ThaiCupid is the right app for a specific person: a man who wants a Thai partner, not a Thai holiday, who can afford a few dollars a month for the duration, and who has the patience to write real messages and wait for real replies. If that is you, read the [Thai girlfriend guide](/guides/thai-girlfriend-guide/) next; it covers what the app cannot, from family visits to sin sod. For that person it is the best tool available, and its slowness and its paywall are features, because they are what keep the freelancers and the tourists on ThaiFriendly.

For a Pattaya visitor with two weeks and no particular agenda, it is the wrong app. ThaiFriendly will give you more in an evening than ThaiCupid gives you in a month, and the [ThaiFriendly review](/guides/thaifriendly-review/) explains what that evening actually looks like.

If you are somewhere in between, and most people are, the honest recommendation is to run both: ThaiFriendly for the trip you are on, ThaiCupid Gold for the one after it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ThaiCupid legit?

Yes. It has operated since 2002 and is owned by Cupid Media, an Australian company that runs a network of dating sites. Individual profiles range from genuine to scam, as on every dating site; the safety section above covers the patterns specific to ThaiCupid.

### How much does ThaiCupid cost?

Gold is around $30 for one month, $60 for three, and $120 for twelve. Platinum is around $35, $75 and $150 for the same periods. Prices vary slightly by country and payment method.

### Can I use ThaiCupid for free?

You can create a profile, browse, and send interests, but you can only exchange messages with paying members. Since most Thai women on the site are free members, a free account for a man is close to useless for actual conversation.

### Is ThaiCupid better than ThaiFriendly?

For a serious relationship, yes: fewer freelancers, better verification, more women looking for a partner. For volume, speed and a Pattaya holiday, ThaiFriendly is far better. Full comparison in the [dating apps in Thailand guide](/guides/dating-apps-thailand/).

### Does ThaiCupid translate messages?

Yes, for Platinum members. Built-in translation runs both ways inside the chat, which is the single most useful reason to pay for Platinum over Gold given how many women on the site write in Thai.

### How do I cancel ThaiCupid?

In your account settings on the website, or through your Apple or Google subscription settings if you subscribed in the app. Cancel before the renewal date; refunds are restricted under Cupid Media's policy.

### Do women on ThaiCupid message first?

Rarely. Thai women on ThaiCupid generally respond rather than initiate, and users treat an unsolicited first message from an unusually attractive profile as a scam signal. This is the opposite of ThaiFriendly, where women writing first is normal.
