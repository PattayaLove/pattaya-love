Massage in Thailand is not a luxury. It is a daily routine for millions of Thais, and in Pattaya a proper one-hour Thai massage at a street shop costs a few hundred baht.

> **Pattaya massage price, in short (ranges, October 2026):** Thai massage about **200-350 THB an hour** at a normal shop, foot massage about **200-350 THB**, oil massage about **300-600 THB**. Beach Road and the busiest tourist strips sit at the top of each range or above it. Spa chains and hotel spas start at roughly **700 THB for two hours** (Health Land) and go up to several thousand baht.

## Massage Prices in Pattaya (2026)

The street-shop figures below are **ranges reported by recent Pattaya guides** (sources at the bottom of the page), compiled on 27 October 2026. They are not our own shop-by-shop survey and not a quote from any single shop. Prices are per hour unless noted.

| Massage Type | Typical price per hour | What It Is |
|---|---|---|
| **Thai Massage** | 200-350 THB | Traditional dry massage. Stretching, pressure points, full body. No oil. Done fully clothed |
| **Foot Massage** | 200-350 THB | Reflexology-style pressure on feet and lower legs, in a reclining chair. 30-minute sessions are common and cheaper |
| **Oil Massage** | 300-600 THB | Full body massage with oil on a table, covered by a towel. Slower and gentler than Thai massage |
| **Aroma / Aromatherapy Massage** | 350-600 THB | Oil massage with scented oils. Often the same treatment as "oil massage" on the board |
| **Aloe Vera / After-Sun Massage** | 250-400 THB | Soothing massage with aloe gel. Popular after a beach day |
| **Head, Neck & Shoulder** | 200-300 THB | Often only 30-45 minutes |
| **Thai Massage with Herbal Compress** | about 500-800 THB, or a small add-on | Heated herbal bundles pressed onto the body, usually combined with Thai massage |

**Longer sessions:** most shops also sell 90 and 120 minutes. Two hours usually costs a little less than double the hourly rate.

**Location matters more than quality.** The same Thai massage that is 200-250 THB on a quiet soi in Naklua or East Pattaya is reported at 300-400 THB on Beach Road and 400-600 THB on Walking Street. A skilled therapist is a skilled therapist, whatever the address.

| Back & shoulder | not listed | 400 THB for 30 min, 800 THB for 60 min |

**Health Land** has a branch on Pattaya Nua Road in Naklua (open daily 10:00-23:00 according to the chain's website). Its two-hour Thai massage works out at roughly the same hourly rate as a Beach Road street shop, which makes it a sensible middle ground. **Let's Relax** is the more polished, higher-priced option and has branches in Pattaya, including an onsen and spa branch.

**Hotel spas** are priced by the hotel, and published ranges differ: roughly 800-2,500 THB per treatment in one 2026 guide, "from about 1,500 THB" in another, and 2,000-4,000 THB at luxury resorts in a third. The [Hilton Pattaya](/stay/hilton-pattaya/) has a spa (eforea); check its current menu before you go. Hotel and resort spas also cluster on [Pratumnak Hill](/areas/pratumnak/) and in the larger Beach Road properties.

What the extra money buys at a spa: a private room, a shower, consistent hygiene, a printed menu and online booking. It does not automatically buy better technique.

**Watch for "++".** If a spa menu prices end in ++, a 10% service charge and 7% VAT are added on top.

## Where Prices Are Lowest

- **[Soi Buakhao](/areas/soi-buakhao/) and the streets around it:** a dense run of shops used by long-stay residents, usually at the bottom of each price range because competition is fierce.
- **Second Road:** plenty of choice, often 50-100 THB an hour above the back streets.
- **Naklua and East Pattaya:** quiet sois with local pricing.
- **[Jomtien](/areas/jomtien/) Beach Road:** simple beachside massage, priced similarly to street shops, and a foot massage with a sea breeze.
- **Beach Road and Walking Street:** convenient, but the most tourist-priced. You pay for the address.

## How to Avoid Overpaying

1. **Look for a price board.** Shops with a printed price list facing the street, bright lighting, plain uniforms and an open front are the straightforward ones.
2. **Ask "how much, total?" before you lie down**, and confirm the duration. Some boards show prices per 30 minutes, not per hour.
3. **A shop with no price board, dim or coloured lighting, a closed door and staff calling to passers-by from outside is a different kind of business.** Walk on.
4. **Carry small notes.** Street shops mostly want cash, and 100 and 50 THB notes make paying and tipping easier.
5. **If a price appears at the end that was never quoted,** do not argue in the street. Pay what was agreed, leave, and if it was genuinely fraudulent, report it to the Tourist Police on **1155**.

## Types of Massage Explained

### Thai Massage (นวดแผนไทย)

The classic. No oil, fully clothed (they provide loose pajama-style clothing). The therapist uses their hands, elbows, knees, and feet to stretch your body and work pressure points. It can be intense. If it is too much, say "bao bao" (softer) or "jep" (pain).

**Best for:** Muscle tension, flexibility, jet lag recovery, general wellness
**Not for:** People with injuries, sunburn, or very low pain tolerance

### Oil Massage (นวดน้ำมัน)

A gentler, more relaxing experience. You undress to your comfort level and are draped with a towel. The therapist uses oil for smooth, flowing strokes. Oil massage on a shop's price board means exactly that: an ordinary oil massage.

**Best for:** Relaxation, stress relief, sore muscles after activities
**Great after:** A day at the beach, hiking, or Muay Thai training

### Foot Massage (นวดเท้า)

Done in a reclining chair, usually in the front of the shop where you can see in from the street. The therapist works on your feet and lower legs using pressure techniques. Some shops start with a hot water foot soak. It is the easiest way to try a new shop before booking a longer session.

**Best for:** Tired feet from walking, an easy introduction to Thai massage

### Herbal Compress Massage

A traditional treatment where heated cloth bundles filled with Thai herbs are pressed against your body. Often combined with a Thai massage; see the price table above.

**Best for:** Deep muscle relaxation, joint pain, an authentic Thai wellness experience

## Etiquette & Tips

### Tipping

- **Standard:** 50-100 THB for a one-hour massage
- **Tip in cash** directly to the therapist, not to the shop
- Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory. There is no percentage convention.

**My own habit:** I always tip at least 50 THB, and if the massage was really good I am happy to give 100 THB.

### What to Expect

1. Choose your massage type at reception and confirm the price
2. Remove shoes, store belongings in a locker
3. Change into provided clothing (Thai massage) or undress to comfort level (oil massage)
4. Therapist will ask about pressure: "strong" or "soft"
5. Relax and communicate if anything is uncomfortable
6. Shower after oil massage (most shops have showers)
7. Pay at reception, tip the therapist separately

Tell the therapist about injuries, recent surgery, pregnancy or back problems before you start, and skip a full-body massage straight after heavy drinking.

### Communication

- **Bao bao** (เบาๆ) = softer/lighter
- **Jep** (เจ็บ) = that hurts
- **Dee** (ดี) = good
- **Nack nack** (หนักๆ) = stronger/harder

## A Massage a Day on a Budget

Many long-term visitors and expats in Pattaya get a massage several times a week. At the bottom of the ranges above, a day could look like this:

- **Morning:** 1-hour foot massage (about 200 THB)
- **After the beach:** 1-hour Thai massage to stretch out (about 200 THB)
- **Evening:** 1-hour oil massage before dinner (about 300 THB)

That is about 700 THB for three professional massages, if you stay at the cheap end. Add a tip of 50-100 THB each time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is a massage in Pattaya?

At a normal street shop, a one-hour Thai or foot massage costs about 200-350 THB and a one-hour oil massage about 300-600 THB (reported ranges, October 2026). Spa chains and hotel spas cost more: see the tables above.

### How much is a Thai massage in Pattaya?

About 200-350 THB for an hour at a street shop, more on Beach Road and Walking Street. Health Land lists 700 THB for two hours.

### How much is a full body massage in Pattaya?

"Full body massage" on a price board usually means either a Thai massage (about 200-350 THB an hour) or an oil massage (about 300-600 THB an hour). Ask which one is meant and confirm the total price first.

### What is the cheapest massage in Pattaya?

A foot massage or a short head, neck and shoulder massage at a back-street shop, from about 200 THB. For a full body treatment, a Thai massage on [Soi Buakhao](/areas/soi-buakhao/) or a quiet soi is the cheapest.

### How much is a couples massage in Pattaya?

We have no sourced couples price. Spas list their treatments on their own menus, so check whether a quoted price is per person or per room before you book.

### How much should I tip?

50-100 THB for an hour is normal. Hand it to the therapist directly. I always give at least 50 THB, and 100 THB when the massage was very good.

### Do I need to book a massage in Pattaya?

Street shops are walk-in. Spa chains and hotel spas are worth booking ahead, especially at weekends and on Thai public holidays.

### Are massage shops open late?

Many shops open around 10:00 and close around midnight. Health Land Pattaya Nua is open 10:00-23:00 according to its own website. Hotel spas tend to close earlier.
