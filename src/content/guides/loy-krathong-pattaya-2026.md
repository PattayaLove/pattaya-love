Loy Krathong is the gentlest night of the Thai year. People gather by the water after dark, light a candle and incense on a small float — a *krathong* — and set it on the water as a thank-you to the water goddess and a way of letting go of the year's bad luck.

In 2026 the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month falls on **Tuesday 24 November**, and that is the night Pattaya celebrates. This guide covers where the city's events are, how to join in respectfully, which krathong to buy, and the one rule about lanterns that you really should know. Last checked: **13 November 2026**.

## Lan Pho Public Park, Naklua

Pattaya City is holding its **main Loy Krathong celebration on 24 November at Lan Pho Public Park in Naklua**, in North Pattaya. The plans were set at a meeting at City Hall on 12 October, chaired by Deputy Mayor Krisana Boonsawat.

On the programme so far:

- A **krathong-making competition** using natural materials
- The **Little Noppamas contest**, the traditional pageant for young participants
- The floating of krathongs after dark

The city says the event is meant to preserve Thai tradition while putting environmental protection and public safety first. The detailed schedule and competition registration had not been published as of 13 November; we will add times once they are out.

Naklua is also one of the better parts of Pattaya for seafood — at the Lan Pho Market on the waterfront you choose your fish or prawns and have them cooked at the restaurants alongside. Make an evening of it. More on the area: [Naklua](/areas/naklua/).

## Koh Larn: Two Days, Eco Theme

The island holds its own two-day programme on **23 and 24 November** at the multipurpose grounds **in front of Wat Mai Samran**, under the theme **"Eco-Friendly Loy Krathong, Local Ways of Koh Larn."**

- **Boat-paddling competitions** on both days
- A **local food activity** on 23 November
- **Free souvenirs** for visitors
- Visitors and residents are encouraged to **wear traditional Thai clothing**

This is the smaller, more local version of the festival, and the eco theme is the point — natural-material krathongs only.

**Watch the ferry.** The last public ferry back from Koh Larn usually leaves around 18:00, well before the krathongs go on the water. If you want to stay for the evening, plan an overnight stay on the island or a speedboat back. Ferries, piers and where to stay: our [Koh Larn guide](/guides/koh-larn-island-guide/).

## How to Join In

You do not need to be Thai or Buddhist to float a krathong. Visitors are welcome at the public celebrations — just take part the way locals do.

1. **Buy a krathong** from a stall near the water. They are sold everywhere on the day.
2. **Light the candle and the incense** sticks on it.
3. **Make a wish** or think of what you want to let go of.
4. **Set it gently on the water** and let it go.

**Etiquette that matters:**

- **Dress modestly**, especially at temple grounds such as Wat Mai Samran. Covered shoulders and knees are the safe choice. Traditional Thai clothing is encouraged on Koh Larn.
- **It is a ceremony, not a party.** Keep the noise down at the waterline, and let families with children go first.
- **Do not take or move other people's krathongs.**
- **Mind the candles and the water's edge.** It is dark, crowded and slippery near the water.

## Choose an Eco-Friendly Krathong

Every year Thai authorities ask people to cut the waste that ends up in rivers and the sea. Bangkok's 2026 green guidelines are a good rule of thumb anywhere:

- **Natural materials only** — banana trunk and banana leaves, which break down quickly. Skip polystyrene foam.
- **Be careful with bread krathongs.** They are sold as fish food, but in closed water such as park ponds the uneaten bread pollutes the water. Bangkok asks people not to use them in its parks.
- **One family, one krathong.** Share one between your group.

Koh Larn's whole programme is built on the same idea, and the natural-materials competition at Lan Pho points the same way.

## Lanterns, Fireworks and Drones: The Airport Warning

Airports of Thailand (AOT) has asked people **not to release sky lanterns, lit or smoke lanterns, fireworks, firecrackers, rockets or balloons near its airports during Loy Krathong, 23–25 November 2026**. The appeal covers six airports: **Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang, Chiang Mai, Mae Fah Luang-Chiang Rai, Phuket and Hat Yai**. Flying a **drone within nine kilometres of an airport requires authorisation**.

AOT says airborne objects can obstruct pilots' view, distract crews at critical moments, and cause severe damage if they enter an engine. The penalties for endangering an aircraft are severe — up to life imprisonment or even the death penalty, or 15 to 20 years and a fine of THB 600,000 to 800,000.

**What this means in Pattaya:** U-Tapao, the airport closest to Pattaya, is not on AOT's list, but aircraft fly into it all the same. Loy Krathong in Pattaya is a festival of floating krathongs, not sky lanterns — leave the lanterns out.

**Flying out that week?** In the north, airlines have cancelled or rescheduled dozens of flights around the festival. AOT advises checking flight status with your airline before you leave and allowing two to three hours to get to the airport.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Loy Krathong 2026 in Pattaya?

Tuesday 24 November 2026, the full-moon night. Koh Larn's programme runs on 23 and 24 November.

### Where is the main celebration in Pattaya?

At Lan Pho Public Park in Naklua, North Pattaya, organised by Pattaya City.

### Can tourists float a krathong?

Yes. Buy one at a stall by the water, light the candle and incense, make a wish and set it on the water. Dress modestly and be respectful — it is a religious and cultural tradition.

### Can I release a sky lantern in Pattaya?

Do not. AOT warns against releasing lanterns near airports from 23 to 25 November, and endangering an aircraft carries severe penalties. Pattaya's celebrations centre on floating krathongs, not lanterns.

### Why do some sites say 25 November?

Some Pattaya listings give the 25th. TAT and Pattaya City both point to 24 November for 2026.

### What should my krathong be made of?

Banana trunk and banana leaves. Avoid foam, and avoid bread krathongs in closed ponds.
