Dating apps work differently in Thailand than they do in Europe, North America or Australia, and nowhere is that more true than in Pattaya. Open ThaiFriendly on your first evening here and you will have more messages within an hour than most people get in a month at home. That is not an exaggeration, and it is also not the whole story.

This guide covers the apps that actually matter in Thailand, what they cost, who uses them, and the part most reviews skip: how the Pattaya dating scene really works, how to tell a freelancer from a girl who just wants dinner, and how to avoid the handful of scams that catch visitors every week.

## The Short Version

| App | Best for | Cost | Pattaya reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ThaiFriendly** | Meeting Thai women fast, casual to serious | Free with limits; Premium ~$25/month, ~$100–120/year | The default app in Pattaya. Huge volume, high freelancer share |
| **ThaiCupid** | Serious relationships, long-term | Gold ~$30/month, Platinum ~$35/month; ~$10–12.50/month on annual plans | Older, more relationship-minded members, many outside Pattaya |
| **Tinder** | Biggest pool, tourists and expats | Free; Plus/Gold priced locally in THB | Busy in Pattaya, mixed intentions, lots of noise |
| **Bumble** | Educated, English-speaking Thais | Free; paid tiers | Works in Bangkok, thin in Pattaya |
| **Hinge** | Relationship-focused | Free; paid tiers | Bangkok only in practice |

If you only install one app for a Pattaya trip, install ThaiFriendly. If you are looking for a partner rather than company for the week, add ThaiCupid. Tinder is worth having as a backup because everybody has it.

## ThaiFriendly — The App Everyone in Pattaya Uses

<a href="https://www.thaifriendly.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">ThaiFriendly</a> has been around since 2010 and claims close to two million members, with roughly a thousand new profiles a day. In Pattaya it is not one option among many; it is the app. Set your location to Pattaya, and the "online now" list on a weekday evening runs into the hundreds.

### How it works

ThaiFriendly is a dating site first and an app second, and it shows. There is no swiping and no matching. You browse profiles by location, age and whether they are online, and you message whoever you like. Anyone can message anyone. That is the whole reason it works so well for visitors: you do not need a Thai woman to have already liked you before you can say hello.

The site is also available in Thai, which is why so many women who speak little English are on it. Expect short messages, heavy use of Google Translate on both sides, and a quick move to LINE once there is mutual interest.

### Free vs Premium

The free account lets you send and receive messages, but with a cooldown between messages and no way to see who has viewed your profile. It is enough to test the water, but in practice the limits bite fast once several conversations are running at once.

Premium removes the limits: unlimited messaging, no ads, higher placement in search results, the ability to see who viewed your profile and who is online, and larger photo views. Before paying anything, take the **7-day free Premium trial** that ThaiFriendly offers through its mobile app after you set up a profile. It converts to a paid month if you do not cancel, so set a reminder, but it gives you a full week of the real product for nothing. After that, the pricing is steep month to month and cheap over a year:

- **1 month:** around $25
- **3 months:** around $50–60
- **12 months:** around $100–120, which works out to $8–10 a month

If you are coming to Pattaya for a two-week holiday, one month of Premium is the honest choice. If you spend months of the year in Thailand, the 12-month plan pays for itself in the first week.

One thing Premium does not do is fix a bad profile. Verify your photo the day you sign up; Thai women on the site filter by the verified badge, and our response rate improved noticeably once it was there. A verified photo, a real first line and a clear idea of what you are looking for will do more for your response rate than any subscription.

We spent a month on the app in Pattaya, including the trial week. The full account, with response rates and the freelancer share by the numbers, is in our [ThaiFriendly review](/guides/thaifriendly-review/).

### Who is actually on it

This is where ThaiFriendly reviews written from a desk in London fall apart. The membership skews young, many women are in their early to mid twenties, and a lot of them are attractive by any standard. That much is true.

What is also true, and specific to Pattaya, is that a meaningful share of the women active on ThaiFriendly in this city are freelancers. Not bar girls attached to a venue, but women who expect to be paid for their time, whether that is dinner and a night or a full week as a travel companion. Some say so in their profile, many do not. We cover how to tell the difference further down, because it matters whether you want that or not.

Outside the freelancer share you will find university students, women working in hotels, malls and massage shops, single mothers from Isaan looking for a stable foreign boyfriend, and a smaller number of women who are genuinely just curious. The mix in Chiang Mai or Khon Kaen looks very different from the mix in Pattaya. Set your search to Pattaya and you get Pattaya.

## ThaiCupid — For Something That Lasts

<a href="https://www.thaicupid.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">ThaiCupid</a> is run by Cupid Media, an Australian company that has operated niche dating sites since 2002. It claims over three million members and, unlike ThaiFriendly, it is unmistakably a relationship site. Profiles are longer, questions about religion, children and whether you want to relocate are part of the setup, and the average age on both sides is higher.

Free members can create a profile, browse and show interest, but can only message members who pay, and since most Thai women on the site are on free accounts, a foreign man on a free account can talk to almost nobody. Gold (around $30 a month, $60 for three months, $120 for a year) unlocks messaging; Platinum (around $35, $75 and $150) adds built-in translation of messages, which is more useful than it sounds when the woman you are talking to writes in Thai. Cupid Media discounts steeply for annual commitments and renews automatically, so buy the length you will use.

The Pattaya angle: ThaiCupid has a dedicated Pattaya search, but the women here are less likely to be freelancers and more likely to be looking for a foreign husband. That cuts both ways. If you want a relationship, this is the better pool. If you want company for Friday night, you will find ThaiCupid slow and the women will find you disappointing. The full breakdown of tiers, prices and who is on it is in our [ThaiCupid review](/guides/thaicupid-review/).

## Tinder — Everyone Has It, Nobody Trusts It

Tinder is the most installed dating app in Thailand, and Pattaya is one of its busiest cities. The pool includes Thai women, other tourists, expats, ladyboys who may or may not say so, and a steady stream of profiles that exist only to move you to LINE and sell you something.

It still works. The advantages are volume and the Passport feature (part of Tinder Plus and Gold), which lets you set your location to Pattaya before you land and arrive with matches waiting. Tinder prices in Thailand are set locally and come to a few hundred baht a month, so buying Gold here is much cheaper than buying it at home.

The disadvantages are noise and intent. Because matching is required, you will spend more time swiping and less time talking than on ThaiFriendly. And because Tinder is where the newest scams show up first, treat any match who wants to talk about crypto, forex or "an investment my uncle told me about" as a scam, without exception.

## Bumble and Hinge — Bangkok Apps

Bumble and Hinge both have real user bases in Thailand, but they are Bangkok apps. The women on them tend to be university-educated, English-speaking and working in offices, and they are dating on their own terms rather than looking for a foreign sponsor. In Bangkok, Sukhumvit and Thonglor especially, both apps are worth your time.

In Pattaya they are thin. You will see a few dozen profiles, many set to Pattaya only because their owner is here for the weekend. If you split your trip between Bangkok and Pattaya, keep them installed for the Bangkok half. If you are here for Pattaya alone, they will not do much.

## Apps to Skip

- **Badoo** still has users in Thailand, but the ratio of bots and hidden-fee profiles is worse than anywhere else on this list.
- **ThaiFlirting, ThaiMatch, TrulyThai and the dozens of "Thai Dating" apps** in the Play Store are smaller copies of ThaiFriendly with fewer members and more aggressive upsells. None of them offers anything the big two do not.
- **Facebook Dating** is technically available in Thailand and technically full of people. In practice it is where profiles go after they have been banned everywhere else.

## The Pattaya Reality: Freelancers, and How to Tell

Every honest guide to dating apps in Pattaya has to address this directly. A freelancer is a woman who is not employed by a bar but who expects payment for her company. In Pattaya, they are a large part of the active ThaiFriendly population and a visible part of Tinder. This is not a moral judgment; it is a description of who is online.

Whether that is a problem depends entirely on what you want. Many visitors are perfectly happy to arrange a few days of company with someone they met on the app and consider it more relaxed and more honest than a [bar fine](/guides/pattaya-nightlife-prices/). Others are here looking for a girlfriend and get burned when they realise the woman they took to dinner expected an envelope at the end of it.

Signs a profile belongs to a freelancer:

- The profile mentions "sponsor", "support", "short time", "long time" or "holiday girlfriend" in any form.
- She is online at 2 or 3 in the morning most nights.
- Her first or second message asks where you are staying and how long you are here.
- She wants to move to LINE within two messages and suggests meeting the same night.
- Photos are professional, heavily filtered, and taken in hotel rooms or nightclubs rather than at home, work or with friends.
- Her profile says she lives in Pattaya but her hometown is listed as somewhere in Isaan and her job is blank or "freelance".

None of these on its own is conclusive. Three or four together are. If you want to avoid the scene, filter for women who list a job, who are online in the daytime, who take a few messages to warm up, and who are happy to meet for lunch or coffee rather than drinks at midnight.

If you do want to meet a freelancer, be an adult about it: agree what you are both expecting before you meet, treat her with the same courtesy you would anyone else, and understand that the arrangement is between the two of you. Rates in 2026 tend to be lower than a Walking Street bar fine plus the arrangement that follows, which is one reason the apps have taken so much business from the bars.

## Scams and Safety

Pattaya dating apps are not dangerous, but they attract a predictable set of scams. Know the patterns and you will not fall for them.

**The sick relative.** After a week of warm messages, her mother is in hospital, her motorbike was stolen or the buffalo is sick. The amount is always just small enough to seem reasonable. Never send money to anyone you have not met, and be very cautious about sending money to anyone you have.

**The crypto investment.** A polished, slightly-too-perfect profile moves you to LINE or WhatsApp, chats about life for a few days, then mentions that she trades gold or crypto on the side and does very well. This is the "pig butchering" scam run by call centres across the border, it has cost visitors to Thailand millions, and the woman in the photos does not exist. Block on the first mention.

**The hotel-room theft.** A minority of freelancers work with a partner or simply wait for you to fall asleep. Keep valuables in the safe, keep your phone on you, and do not leave drinks unattended. This is standard Pattaya advice, and it applies to app dates as much as to bars.

**Undisclosed ladyboys.** ThaiFriendly has a separate section for ladyboy profiles and Tinder has gender options, but not everyone uses them honestly. If it matters to you, a video call before meeting settles it, and asking politely is not considered rude in Thailand.

**Fake profiles and stolen photos.** Reverse-image-search any profile that looks like a model shoot. On ThaiFriendly, the verified-photo badge is worth filtering for.

Practical safety, whatever the app: meet in public the first time, do a short video call before you meet, tell someone where you are going, and do not hand over your passport to anyone for any reason.

## Practical Details for App Dates in Pattaya

**LINE.** Every conversation that goes anywhere moves to LINE, Thailand's messaging app. Install it before you arrive and set up an ID. Do not share it with everyone who asks in the first message; that is where the spam starts.

**Data.** You will be on the apps constantly. A [Thai SIM or eSIM](/guides/thailand-sim-esim/) with unlimited data costs less than one ThaiFriendly Premium month and saves you from hotel Wi-Fi.

**Your hotel.** Most Pattaya hotels are guest-friendly, meaning a visitor can stay overnight at no extra charge, but the upmarket ones charge a joiner fee of 500 to 1,500 THB, and a few refuse guests altogether. Check before you book. Our [guest-friendly hotels guide](/guides/guest-friendly-hotels-pattaya/) lists verified no-fee hotels by area, including the Hilton, and explains what happens at the front desk.

**Where to meet.** Terminal 21 and Central Pattaya are the default first-date suggestions and for good reason: air-conditioned, public, food on every floor, easy to leave. For something better, a sunset drink at one of the [rooftop bars](/guides/rooftop-dining/) costs 250 to 500 THB a cocktail and makes an impression. Thai women generally prefer to eat Thai, so a good [local restaurant](/guides/pattaya-food-guide/) beats a steakhouse.

**Timing.** Daytime dates filter out most of the freelancer scene by themselves. A woman with a job who agrees to lunch at 1pm on a Tuesday is telling you something.

## Profile Tips That Work With Thai Women

The advice that works at home mostly works here, with a few local adjustments.

- **Smile in your photos.** Thai culture reads a serious face as unfriendly or angry. The moody black-and-white shot that works on Hinge in Berlin does nothing here.
- **Show your life, not your body.** Shirtless mirror selfies signal exactly one thing. Photos of you eating, travelling or with friends signal that you are a normal person with a life.
- **Say what you want.** "Here for two weeks, looking for someone to explore Pattaya with" is honest and gets honest replies. Pretending to want a wife when you want company for the weekend wastes everybody's time and gets you a reputation on the Thai side faster than you think.
- **Keep messages short and simple.** English is a second language for most women here. Three short sentences beat a paragraph. Avoid idioms and sarcasm; both are lost in translation.
- **Do not open with anything sexual.** It gets you blocked by the women you want and attracts the ones you were trying to filter out.
- **Mention that you have been to Thailand before, if you have.** Women on these apps have learned to be wary of first-timers who fall in love in three days and vanish in four.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ThaiFriendly free?

Yes, with limits. Free members can send and receive messages, but with a waiting period between messages and without seeing who viewed their profile. Premium removes the limits and costs around $25 for one month or $100–120 for a year.

### Is ThaiFriendly legit or a scam?

The site itself is legitimate and has operated since 2010. Individual profiles are another matter; like every dating app in Thailand it has fake profiles and scammers, and the safety section above covers the common patterns. Filter for verified photos, do a video call before meeting, and never send money.

### Which dating app is most used in Pattaya?

ThaiFriendly, by a wide margin, followed by Tinder. ThaiCupid has a real presence but a smaller and older one. Bumble and Hinge barely register in Pattaya.

### Can I use Tinder in Thailand before I arrive?

Yes, with Tinder Plus or Gold, which include the Passport feature. Set your location to Pattaya a few days before you fly and you will land with matches. Buy the subscription after you arrive; Thai pricing is lower.

### Do Thai women on dating apps expect money?

Some do, and in Pattaya a significant share of active profiles belong to freelancers who expect to be paid for their time. Many others do not. The section on spotting freelancers above explains how to tell the difference, and being clear about what you want from the start avoids most misunderstandings.

### What is a joiner fee?

A charge some hotels add when an overnight guest who is not registered to the room stays over, typically 500 to 1,500 THB in Pattaya. Most mid-range Pattaya hotels do not charge one. Our [guest-friendly hotels guide](/guides/guest-friendly-hotels-pattaya/) has the verified list.

## Final Thoughts

Dating apps in Thailand are genuinely easier than dating apps almost anywhere else, and Pattaya is the most extreme version of that. Women will message you first, they will reply, and they will meet you. The catch is not getting a date; it is knowing what kind of date you are getting.

Use ThaiFriendly for volume and speed, ThaiCupid if you want something serious, keep Tinder as a backup, and spend ten minutes learning the freelancer signals before you start messaging. Do that, and the apps will give you a better and more honest experience of Pattaya than the bars ever could.
